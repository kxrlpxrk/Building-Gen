The full crossing hall has an 8 m secondary gable centred at 9 m, matching the host's 42° pitch. The offset projection uses a 3 m bay at 4 m, depth 4 m, 48° pitch and +0.2 m relative eaves. The third fixture uses the existing BG-05 seeded generator and finds a validated exposed facade bay without changing its footprint, doors, windows or architectural features.

These are inspection evidence, not pixel-golden assertions or physical Safari performance certification. Deterministic geometry tests separately verify analytic valleys, roof-skin coverage, winding/nondegeneracy, supported cutoffs and facade closure. The earlier BG-04D.2 roof fixtures are still tested and captured independently.
